# Non Goals

ChopDot is not trying to become every financial product at once.

## Current Non Goals

- Custody by default.
- Escrow or automatic payouts in normal product copy.
- DAO governance claims.
- A generic bank, wallet, or card dashboard.
- A public proof product before the user journey works.
- A protocol console for users.
