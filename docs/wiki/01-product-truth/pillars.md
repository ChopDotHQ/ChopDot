# Product Pillars

ChopDot work must improve at least one pillar without damaging the others.

## Pillars

- Catch: capture the money moment.
- Management: make group state and next action obvious.
- Payout: support the right payment path.
- History: close with a record the group trusts later.

## Rule

A feature that only exposes infrastructure or adds explanation without reducing friction is not a product improvement.
