ChopDot must first complete the ordinary job people already understand, then
make it more trustworthy, and only then promote unproven ideas. This page is a
map, not product law, current priority, or implementation evidence.

## Product composition layers

1. **Category baseline.** Cover the applicable familiar outcome at least as
   clearly as the strongest relevant conventional product or null workflow in
   the [benchmark baseline](../../../product/benchmark-baseline.md). A missing
   basic cannot be offset by a differentiated feature or high product score.
2. **ChopDot differentiation.** Improve the baseline with exact and distinct
   money states, receiver-confirmed truth, participant-held history, humane
   exceptions, privacy, recovery, and cross-rail coordination without exposing
   infrastructure or bureaucracy.
3. **Experiments.** Keep OCR, automatic item extraction, novel rails,
   personhood, new entrances, and other hypotheses bounded until named evidence
   promotes them. Experiments may fail or be removed.

The layers describe what an accepted experience contains. They do not select a
single Home action. One obvious action is chosen from the person's observed
state and job: creating a group, responding to an invitation, capturing a
spend, contributing, confirming, returning, or recovering may each require a
different action.

## Delivery phases

| Phase | Outcome | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Grounding and authority | One current hierarchy, benchmark floor, comparable priority judgment, and one money/membership authority. | Every active user-facing card names baseline coverage, differentiation, phase, evidence, owner, failure, and exit. |
| 1. Baseline usable product | First shared group and the normal pot/trip/couple journey cover entry, people, expense/capture, split, status, payment or external record, exception, confirmation, close, return, export, and reconnect. | One organizer and two participants complete the benchmarked production journey without developer coaching. |
| 2. ChopDot trust differentiation | Exact money, distinct payment states, receiver-confirmed closeout, append-only correction, participant-held history, delivery, revocation, and recovery improve the normal job without visible ceremony. | Participants converge after exception, restart, reorder, and recovery while the baseline remains at least as clear. |
| 3. Named money cultures | Spend Card, savings circle, emergency pot, and community fund complete their benchmark/null-workflow outcomes on the same authority core. | Each mode passes its relevant benchmark and adds no parallel money, membership, payment, recovery, or closeout authority. |
| 4. Assured immutable release | Product, accessibility, privacy, security, contract, recovery, offline, responsive, and release gates pass for one deterministic candidate promoted byte-identically. | Implemented, tested, committed, pushed, candidate built, staged, promoted, reachable, and user owned are each proved. |
| 5. Real use and durable knowledge | Real participants complete the journeys; same-task comparisons record wins and losses; portable cited recall points to the accepted outcome. | User proven and knowledge known are independently true, and observed use selects the next phase. |

These are acceptance phases, not permission to hide a missing Phase 1 basic
until every internal Phase 0 mechanism is perfect. Work may proceed in parallel,
but later-phase claims cannot compensate for an earlier-phase failed outcome.
