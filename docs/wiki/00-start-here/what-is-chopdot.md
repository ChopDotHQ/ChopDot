# What Is ChopDot

ChopDot is a group-money coordination app.

It helps groups capture money moments, show who owes or has paid, confirm what was received, and close with a readable saved record.

## Product Spine

```text
I paid / I need to collect
-> ChopDot captures the moment
-> friends get one obvious action
-> payment is marked or recorded
-> receiver confirms what arrived
-> group closes with a readable saved record
```

## Four Pillars

- Catch: capture expenses, receipts, payments, requests, and commitments.
- Management: show the current group state and next action.
- Payout: move or record money through the right payment path.
- History: preserve a trusted record the group can return to.
