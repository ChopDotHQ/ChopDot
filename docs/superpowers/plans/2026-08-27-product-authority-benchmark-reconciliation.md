# ChopDot product authority and benchmark reconciliation

**Kind:** plan
**Status:** completed locally; logical commit pending mixed-worktree attribution
**Owner:** product assurance
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-27
**Applies to:** `chopdot-v1-launch`
**Authority:** bounded documentation, Cockpit, loop, and skill repair; it cannot
change `PRODUCT_TRUTH.md`, claim E2 competitor proof, implement product code, or
claim release completion

## Goal

Make one coherent product operating system for the launch worktree:

```text
product law
-> current benchmark floor
-> phased product outcomes
-> Cockpit packages and decisions
-> tracked agent judgment and design methods
-> thin machine-local skill loaders
-> objective validation and evidence
```

The product experience must first cover the accepted category basics evidenced
by conventional group-money applications. ChopDot differentiation is layered on
top. Unproven ideas remain experiments. No scenario, skill, plan, generated
view, or knowledge backend may silently promote one example into the whole
product.

## Product layers

1. **Category baseline:** familiar jobs must be complete and at least as clear
   as the strongest relevant conventional or null workflow.
2. **ChopDot differentiation:** receiver-confirmed truth, participant-held
   history, humane exception handling, recovery, privacy, and cross-rail
   coordination improve the baseline without adding visible bureaucracy.
3. **Experiments:** receipt automation, new rails, novel modes, personhood, and
   other unproven ideas stay bounded until evidence promotes them.

These layers answer what an experience contains. They do not replace the
delivery phases in the roadmap.

## Loop contract

- **Expected outcome:** a newly hydrated agent can explain ChopDot's product
  layers, delivery phases, current live priority, benchmark evidence boundary,
  and next proof without inventing a universal journey.
- **Owner:** product assurance; product owns later benchmark and strategy
  decisions; an independent reviewer must assess the final source set.
- **Maximum repair budget:** two source-edit passes and one independent semantic
  audit. Repeating the same failing assertion without changing the hypothesis
  does not count as repair.
- **Failure outcome:** same-level source conflict, missing benchmark provenance,
  invalid context hashes, or baseline-free product claims block completion.
- **Exit:** focused and full governance validation passes; all changed paths are
  attributed; E2 competitor and real-user work remains explicitly open; the
  accepted slice is ready for a logical commit without unrelated dirty paths.
