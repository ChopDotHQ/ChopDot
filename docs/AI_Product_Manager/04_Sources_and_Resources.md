# AI Product Management: Sources and Resources

This document compiles the tools, frameworks, and academic/industry references cited in the AI Product Management literature for further exploration.

## The AI Tech Stack: Growth and Operations

### 1. Data Storage
- **Relational Databases:** Best for structured data and straightforward querying.
- **Data Warehouses:** Centralizes structured data for advanced analytics and BI (e.g., Snowflake, Amazon Redshift, Google BigQuery).
- **Data Lakes:** Cost-effective storage for massive amounts of raw, unstructured data.
- **Lakehouses:** Combines the flexibility of data lakes with the data management features of warehouses (e.g., Databricks).

### 2. MLOps Platforms
Tools for building, tracking, deploying, and maintaining ML pipelines:
- **Databricks MLflow:** Open-source platform for the complete ML lifecycle.
- **Google TensorFlow Extended (TFX):** End-to-end platform for deploying production ML pipelines.
- **Amazon SageMaker:** Fully managed infrastructure tools for training and deploying models.
- **Weights & Biases:** Tool for managing versions and experiments.

### 3. Early Signal & Growth Hacking Tools
- **Customer Data Platforms (CDPs):** Collect and unify customer data (Segment, Klaviyo, Hightouch).
- **Customer Engagement Platforms (CEPs):** Facilitate in-app messaging and walk-throughs (UserPilot, AppCues, Intercom).
- **Product Analytics:** Track user behavior funnels (Amplitude, Pendo, Mixpanel, Heap).
- **A/B Testing:** Frameworks for testing features and AI variants (VWO, Optimizely, AB Tasty).

## AI Ethics and Governance Frameworks

- **The European Commission's Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI:** Focuses on human autonomy, prevention of harm, fairness, and explicability.
- **US Department of Defense (DoD) Ethical Principles for AI:** Responsible, equitable, traceable, reliable, and governable.
- **National AI Initiative Office:** Advancing Trustworthy AI.
- **Algorithmic Justice League:** Research and advocacy against algorithmic bias.
- **AI for Good:** Initiatives spanning the UN (ITU), DataKind, and Omdena focusing on humanitarian and social impact.

## General AI Information
- **Gartner Magic Quadrant:** For evaluating market position (Leaders, Challengers, Visionaries, Niche players).
- **McKinsey Technology Trends Outlook:** Reports on the economic impact of Applied AI, Computer Vision, and NLP.
