# AI Specializations and Roles

The AI Product Management landscape is evolving rapidly. While traditional software companies have standard PM roles, AI products demand specialized expertise. *The AI Product Playbook* defines three distinct AI PM Personas, while *The AI Product Manager's Handbook* outlines the technical roles required to build the "AI Dream Team."

## The 3 AI PM Personas

These are not necessarily separate job titles, but different areas of focus within the AI PM landscape.

### 1. AI-Experiences PM: Shaping User Interaction
- **Focus:** The champion of the user experience. Translates user needs into AI-powered solutions.
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Designing intuitive AI interactions (e.g., how AI outputs are presented, handling edge cases).
  - Managing user expectations around probabilistic outputs (e.g., designing fallbacks when the AI is uncertain).
  - Championing ethical and responsible AI practices (mitigating bias, ensuring fairness and transparency).
  - Designing and running A/B tests to evaluate the real-world impact of AI features.
- **Skills:** Deep user empathy, UX collaboration, prompt engineering, understanding of GenAI Evals.

### 2. AI-Builder PM: Architecting the Foundation
- **Focus:** The architect of the AI infrastructure. Builds the platforms, models, and frameworks that empower other teams.
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Defining the AI platform strategy and roadmap.
  - Managing AI infrastructure and MLOps (CI/CD, IaC).
  - Overseeing the development of reusable "foundational" AI models.
  - Establishing data governance, privacy, and compliance policies.
  - Tracking system health, data drift, and model retraining schedules.
- **Skills:** Deep technical expertise, infrastructure knowledge (cloud, edge), data engineering collaboration.

### 3. AI-Enhanced PM: Supercharging the Workflow
- **Focus:** The power user of AI. Leverages AI tools to improve their own product management workflows and decision-making.
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Identifying opportunities to automate PM tasks (e.g., market research, competitive analysis).
  - Synthesizing user feedback and identifying user segments using clustering tools.
  - Drafting PRDs and roadmaps using Generative AI.
  - Driving the adoption of AI tools within the product team.
- **Skills:** Adaptability, learning agility, prompt engineering, critical evaluation of AI outputs to avoid over-reliance.

## The AI / ML Dream Team

Building a successful AI product requires a highly specialized, cross-functional team.

1. **AI Product Manager:** The central hub aligning business goals, user needs, and technical feasibility.
2. **AI/ML/Data Strategist:** A consultative role that helps establish workflows, ethics principles, and early tech stack decisions.
3. **Data Engineer:** Builds the data pipelines (ETL) and infrastructure (Data Lakes, Data Warehouses) required to feed clean data to the models.
4. **Data Analyst:** Analyzes the data to identify trends, create dashboards, and extract business insights.
5. **Data Scientist:** Experiments with algorithms, engineers features, and builds/trains the core ML models.
6. **ML Engineer (MLOps):** Takes the trained models and integrates them into production, managing deployment, scaling, and continuous monitoring.
7. **Software/Full-Stack Engineers:** Integrate the AI/ML outputs into the broader product architecture and UI.
8. **UX Designers/Researchers:** Craft the user interface, minimizing friction and designing around AI uncertainty and edge cases.
