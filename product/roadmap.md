# ChopDot Public Beta Roadmap

1. Govern the exact launch worktree and isolate its dependencies.
2. Make One Chop Core the production authority.
3. Complete identity, membership, encrypted delivery and recovery.
4. Ship the receipt-first normal loop and group-card home.
5. Complete payment/history and all four named modes.
6. Freeze, assure, stage and promote one immutable testnet release.

Do not branch into custody, card issuing, automatic debit/FX, credit, yield,
mainnet, token governance, or a private backend during this release train.
