# ChopDot Public Beta Roadmap

**Kind:** decision
**Status:** active
**Owner:** product
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-27
**Applies to:** `chopdot-v1-launch`
**Authority:** current operator ordering only; each priority must satisfy the Cockpit outcome contract and cannot define a universal participant action

## Product composition layers

These layers describe what a user-facing experience contains. They are not a
release schedule and do not select one universal first action.

1. **Category baseline:** complete the familiar job at least as clearly as the
   strongest relevant conventional product or null workflow recorded in
   `product/benchmark-baseline.md`.
2. **ChopDot differentiation:** improve that baseline with trustworthy money
   states, receiver confirmation, participant-held history, humane exceptions,
   privacy, recovery, and cross-rail coordination without visible bureaucracy.
3. **Experiments:** keep unproven capture automation, rails, modes, personhood,
   and other hypotheses bounded until their named evidence promotes them.

## Delivery phases

### Phase 0 — Grounding and authority

- Keep exact-root context, Product Truth, Cockpit decisions, OutcomePackets,
  and portable cited recall fail closed.
- Complete P-013's conventional category floor and the E2 walkthroughs needed
  for the Phase 1 normal journey. Later-mode E2 work remains explicit on P-013
  and the relevant mode cards until their accepting phase.
- Apply comparative Product Portfolio Judgment before accepting or changing a
  priority. Product score remains admission evidence, never ranking evidence.
- Preserve one signed money and membership authority through P-034.

**Exit:** every active user-facing card names its benchmark coverage,
differentiated outcome, evidence state, owner, proof, failure, and exit, and the
Phase 1 comparator/null walkthroughs are current enough for same-task use.

### Phase 1 — Baseline usable group-money product

- Repair P-035 so a first participant creates one shared group and admits
  another intentionally without infrastructure coaching.
- Repair P-022 so empty and returning Home states expose one working contextual
  action rather than a dashboard or universal receipt entrance.
- Prove the normal pot/trip/couple journey through `src/main.tsx`: create/join,
  people, expense/capture, splits, balances, payment handoff or external record,
  exceptions, confirmation, close, return, export, and offline/reconnect.

**Exit:** one organizer and two participants finish the benchmarked normal
journey through the production entrypoint without developer coaching.

### Phase 2 — ChopDot trust differentiation

- Re-prove exact money, distinct payment states, receiver-confirmed closeout,
  append-only correction, participant-held history, encrypted delivery,
  revocation, fresh-device recovery, and social re-grant.
- Demonstrate that these boundaries reduce ambiguity without adding visible
  protocol or compliance ceremony.

**Exit:** the same participants converge after exception, restart, reorder, and
recovery while a conventional baseline task remains at least as clear.

### Phase 3 — Complete the named money cultures

- Spend Card covers transaction/receipt matching, mismatch, duplicate, late
  receipt, refund, reversal, privacy, export, and normal close.
- Savings circle covers rules, order, contribution, delay/default, payout,
  recipient confirmation, one advance, replacement/exit, recovery, and export.
- Emergency pot covers private request, trusted roles/threshold, contribution,
  approval, release, confirmation/dispute, recovery, and redacted record.
- Community fund covers roles, contributions, proposal, threshold decision,
  release, confirmation, steward handoff/recovery, and report/export.

**Exit:** every mode passes its benchmark/null-workflow comparison and adds no
second money, membership, payment, recovery, or closeout authority.

### Phase 4 — Assured immutable public-testnet release

- Clear governance, merge-boundary, dependency, visual, accessibility, privacy,
  security, contract, recovery, performance, offline, responsive, and release
  findings.
- Commit and push logical reviewed slices without merging outside the approval
  envelope.
- Freeze one deterministic candidate, deploy the same bounded recovery-contract
  behavior on Products Devnet and Paseo, stage one CAR/CID, and promote those
  identical bytes without rebuilding.
- Verify every advertised surface and rollback, then transfer the names and
  independently read back ownership.

**Exit:** implemented, tested, committed, pushed, candidate built, staged,
promoted, reachable, and user owned are independently true for one fingerprint.

### Phase 5 — Real use and durable knowledge

- Complete real organizer-plus-two-participant acceptance for the normal
  journey and every named mode.
- Re-run same-journey category comparisons, record losses as product gaps, and
  promote only evidence-backed differentiators.
- Refresh the exact-worktree Repo Graph and current portable knowledge backend;
  require citations to the accepted root, branch, commit, benchmark, and
  outcome without fallback.

**Exit:** user proven and knowledge known are independently true; the next
product phase is selected from observed use rather than an inherited scenario.

Do not branch into custody, card issuing, automatic debit/FX, credit, yield,
mainnet, token governance, or a private backend during this release train.
