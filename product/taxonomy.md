# ChopDot Product Taxonomy

## Pillars

- `Catch`: capture payment, receipt, intent, request, or contribution at the moment it happens
- `Management`: show who is involved, who is next, what is open, and what needs review
- `Payout`: help the right person pay or be paid through the right path
- `History`: close with a saved readable record
- `Native Stack`: future infrastructure that should stay invisible to normal users
- `Quality`: tests, cockpit, scorecards, language gates, and readiness evidence
