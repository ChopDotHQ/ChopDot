# ChopDot Priority Signals

Use these signals when reviewing cards.

## High Priority

- reduces first useful action time
- removes manual entry
- makes one next action obvious
- clarifies who paid, who received, or what remains open
- protects privacy in sensitive money contexts
- prevents false readiness claims

## Medium Priority

- improves repeated group workflows
- strengthens evidence and handoff quality
- supports future native stack adoption without user-facing complexity
- improves agent or operator continuity

## Low Priority

- polish without changing the journey
- optional surfaces not tied to a current user job
- infrastructure not yet pulled by product need

## Demotion Signals

- adds explanation before action
- only changes labels
- exposes internal architecture
- makes a simple group-money action feel like administration
- cannot be evaluated through screenshots or real UI use
