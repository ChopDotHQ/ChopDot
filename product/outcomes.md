# ChopDot Product Outcomes

## OUT-001 - Capture the money moment

Users should start from "I just paid" or "I need to collect", not from a blank expense form.

Success signals:

- first useful shared state under 30 seconds
- receipt/photo/link/amount capture before item editing
- no normal UI internal language
- clear friend action generated from the capture

## OUT-002 - One-action friend participation

Friends should act without becoming app operators.

Success signals:

- no-app links open directly to the relevant action
- the friend sees amount, receiver, context, and done state
- no full account setup before a low-risk first action

## OUT-003 - Trustworthy confirmation

The group should know what was paid, what was received, who is next, and what remains open.

Success signals:

- mark paid is distinct from confirmed received
- receiver confirmation is clear and scoped to one matching item
- close only happens when items are confirmed, delayed, waived, or annotated

## OUT-004 - Repeated group modes

Savings circles, emergency pots, and community funds should feel like native ChopDot uses of the same commitment loop.

Success signals:

- each mode has one obvious next action
- roles have scoped visibility and actions
- privacy-sensitive records default to redacted output

## OUT-005 - Honest native stack adoption

Polkadot-native capabilities should reduce friction or increase trust without turning the app into infrastructure theater.

Success signals:

- live `.dot` and host-native gaps remain visible as blocked or setup required
- normal UI never exposes native stack terminology
- native work is tested as adapter behavior, not product truth

## OUT-006 - Product flywheel

The team should know what to build next, why it matters, and what evidence exists.

Success signals:

- cockpit board is useful within 30 seconds
- cards link journeys, screens, tests, decisions, and evidence
- history is append-only
- future agents can resume from generated product context
