How Insights worksInsights
Demo

Useful patterns, not new money truth

Insights reads accepted records that you are currently allowed to see. It dedupes corrected versions and compares only like-for-like currencies and periods.

It never changes an expense, balance, request, payment, savings position or group.

No people scores, trust rankings or hidden cross-currency conversions.
