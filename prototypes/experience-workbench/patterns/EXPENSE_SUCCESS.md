# Golden Pattern — Expense Success

Established by Journey 05 Add Expense V1.

## Purpose
Confirm the expense was recorded and show its immediate personal effect.

## Required content
- Expense name
- Total amount
- Payer
- Date
- User's share or position
- Back to group
- Add another

## Rules
- Important expense creation does not end with toast-only feedback.
- The success state shows what changed.
- The primary exit returns to the updated group.
