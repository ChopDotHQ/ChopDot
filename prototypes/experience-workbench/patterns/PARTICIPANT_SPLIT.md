# Golden Pattern — Participants and Split

Established by Journey 05 Add Expense V1.

## Purpose
Include the right people and choose how the cost is divided.

## Default
Everyone included. Equal split.

## Supported methods
- Equal
- Exact amounts
- Weighted shares

## Rules
- Participants and method are separate decisions.
- Always show the resulting personal amounts.
- Custom totals must visibly reconcile to the expense total.
- Invalid totals block completion and explain the remaining difference.
- Excluded members remain visible as excluded rather than silently disappearing.
